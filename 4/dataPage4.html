Wolf-Rayet Star

A giant star that is stripped bare, leaving behind a core and cloud of gas.

MAGE: A Magnetar | IMAGE CREDIT: NASA/JPL-Caltech

Magnetar

A neutron star with the strongest magnetic field to ever exist.

IMAGE: A Yellow Hypergiant | IMAGE CREDIT: ESO/Digitized Sky Survey 2

Yellow Hypergiant

One of the rarest stars to exist, the yellow hypergiant is very unstable compared to its counterparts.

IMAGE: A Illustration of a Brown Dwarf | IMAGE CREDIT: NASA-JPL, Caltech

Brown Dwarf

The in-between of a planet and a star. Brown dwarfs are essnetially formed like stars, but lack the necessary mass in order to act like one. As such, these stars behave more like a planet.
