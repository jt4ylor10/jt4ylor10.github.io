Evolved Giant Star: Black Dwarf

IMAGE: A theoretical Black Dwarf | IMAGE CREDIT: Baperookamo

Dusty's Log Entry:

  • Type: hypothetical remnant
  • Temperature: 2.7 K
  • Mass: 0.6 Suns
  • Lifespan: forever
  • What's Next: nothing

A black dwarf is a theoretical star which explains life after a white dwarf. As a white dwarf cools off, it will eventually have cooled completely. At that point, it's a black dwarf which gives off no light and is the same temperature as the space around it. A white dwarf cools extremely slowly, with a lifespan of at least hundreds of trillions of years. The universe is about 13.4 billion years old, as such, a black dwarf can't even exist yet. The oldest of white dwarfs are still glowing today, but a black dwarf is a highly logical theory for the future of all sunlike stars.

There are no examples, except for illustrations.
